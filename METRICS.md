\# Metrics



Important metrics for AI Spend Doctor:



\## Product metrics



\- Number of audits completed

\- Number of leads captured

\- Average estimated savings

\- Form completion rate

\- Deployment uptime



\## Business metrics



\- Visitor to lead conversion rate

\- Number of teams interested

\- Potential monthly savings found

\- Repeat usage from teams



These metrics can help improve the product and understand user interest.
