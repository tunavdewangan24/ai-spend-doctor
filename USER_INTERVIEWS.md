\# User Interviews



For this assignment, I considered possible users like students, startup founders, and small teams using multiple AI tools.



\## Observations



\- Users may not track all AI subscriptions properly.

\- Small teams may pay for extra seats they do not use.

\- API spending can increase without proper monitoring.

\- A simple audit report can help users understand where money is going.



\## Future interview questions



\- Which AI tools do you currently pay for?

\- Do you track monthly AI spending?

\- Have you ever paid for unused seats?

\- Would you use a simple audit tool to find savings?
